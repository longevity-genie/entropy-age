## Ranked findings

I treated a negative point estimate, an interval below zero, and a result robust to donor or animal resampling as three different findings. I used relative entropy for the main inventory where it was available, and named the measure where it differed. Table paths below are relative to the repository root. The review was read-only.

1. **CHERRY-PICK · CONTRADICTION — The division claim changes when the other tissues, cell fractions, and culture results are counted.**  
   **Text.** `manuscript/paper.md`, “The shield goes with cell division”: “In series where batch cannot explain the contrast, the shield is larger where cells divide.” `docs/CLAIMS.md`, C2: “The mouse-array ordering withdrawn in R92 is now seen on clean data.” `ARTIFACT_TEXT.md`, **intro**, “The shield goes with cell division”: “The lines of evidence point the same way.” The web **history** tab also says “No shield where cells do not divide,” while **intro** says “Cells that do not divide are less shielded, not unshielded.”  
   **Evidence.** The strongest clean tissue comparison is two tissues in one male mouse cohort: colon −0.189 and hippocampus −0.078 SD, difference −0.111 [−0.222, −0.085], `data/output/jena_rrbs/contrast.csv`, housekeeping `kl_slope`. The paired human result is specific to NeuN−/SOX10− glia: −0.055 [−0.121, −0.007], versus −0.003 [−0.044, +0.037] for the oligodendrocyte lineage and −0.044 [−0.108, +0.015] in the second sorted cohort, `nuclei_paired/paired.csv` and `nuclei_paired_gse66351/paired.csv`, housekeeping `kl_slope`. The web headline cites the first contrast and gives the second estimate in **intro**, but leaves out the interval in its headline summaries. GSE280465 gives no resolved ordering among buccal cells, dried blood spots, and PBMCs with chip held, `cross_tissue_chip/paired.csv`, housekeeping `dispersion`. GTEx testis is near zero while slowly renewing kidney and ovary are both about −0.09, `gtex_tissues/point_estimates.csv`, housekeeping `kl_slope`, `covariates`. Thompson RRBS muscle and blood are both about −0.08, `mouse_rrbs_tissues/aging_gaps.csv`. Meer RRBS cortex and the Jena hippocampus retain measurable gaps. GSE225166 complete-coverage RRBS resolves lung, with heart and cortex intervals crossing zero. Huang’s renewing-minus-post-mitotic difference moves from −0.19 to +0.02 [−0.06, +0.19] with chip held, `mouse_tissues/contrasts.csv`. The separate consortium analysis puts liver with the larger gaps and kidney with the smaller ones, `RUN_LOG.md` R118.  
   **Verdict — cell division: supported in part.** The paper supplies much of this contrary evidence elsewhere. C2 and the web summaries turn specific contrasts into an ordering that the full inventory does not establish.  
   **Corrected sentence.** “The housekeeping gap is larger in colon than hippocampus in one mouse cohort and in one glial fraction than paired neurons in one human cohort, and a gap per doubling recurs in cultured cells. Across tissues, the measured gaps do not follow a consistent turnover ranking.”

3. **UNSUPPORTED · CONTRADICTION — Tet3 is a bulk-tissue perturbation result at one age.**

5. **CHERRY-PICK · CONTRADICTION — Culture distinguishes detectable drift from arrest more clearly than it distinguishes selection from maintenance.**  
   **Text.** `ARTIFACT_TEXT.md`, **history**, “The shield's share grows with age in vivo but not in culture”: “Flat over 136 doublings in a dish; growing with age in mouse blood and liver.” Its archived C3 says “Status holds against selection as the main cause.” **Next** says “the data in hand cannot separate them,” as do the current paper and `docs/CLAIMS.md` C3. The web **intro** says “Time alone does not move promoter methylation in culture.”  
   **Evidence.** Endicott F-change −0.14 [−0.33, +0.06] per 10 doublings, rebuilt −0.18 [−0.39, +0.04] with near-zero reference coupling; Sturm −0.055 [−0.21, +0.10], rebuilt −0.025 [−0.08, +0.03]. See `passaged_cells*/coupling_across_lines.csv` and R94. Chip explains about 77% of Endicott doublings. Its +0.47/+0.49 short trajectories sit near the assay floor. In R111, replicative senescence F2 was −0.58 [−0.60, −0.55] across 12 chips; X-ray and RAS arms had too little drift to estimate F2 and their duration is unrecorded. In R136, fibroblasts and stromal cells give F2 −0.39, leave-one-donor-out range −0.46 to −0.36, with little drift after irradiation or doxorubicin. The absolute-coefficient gaps look negative in all arms, including those at the floor, `senescence_donors/matched_gaps.csv`. In R112, two informative donors show no detectable monthly drift, the third is confounded by chip position, and an EPICv2 donor was excluded. One absolute-slope contrast is nominally positive and one negative despite the relative-entropy intervals all including zero, `arrest_timecourse/gaps.csv`. R89 finds positive **time** and **division** coefficients for look-alike drift in both culture series, `culture_rates/rates.csv`, housekeeping B about +0.015 per 10 doublings and +0.0012/+0.0052 per 10 days. Positive division evidence also includes R124/125, R130, R133 and R135. The claim that mortal BJ cells give “no clear reading” omits the same-chip arm’s negative donor/culture interval, although the earlier batch changes sign by metric.  
   **Other tests bearing on selection.** Clonal haematopoiesis shows no resolved change, including rebuilt and conversion-adjusted variants, `chip_methylation*/effects.csv`, R25/R99/R126; clone fractions are unavailable. HSCT F was −0.039 for housekeeping, −0.058 for essential, and −0.057 for non-essential, `hsct_bottleneck/effects.csv`, R57. Petkovich CR blood F +0.025 (*p* = 0.002) shrank to +0.008 (*p* = 0.07) with openness matched, `cr_chromatin/intervention_effects.csv`. CR liver −0.008 (*p* = 0.77); short CR blood points the other way, core-essential F −0.09 with within-chip reference-refitting *p* = 0.16, and rebuilt −0.08, *p* = 0.36; its 12-week aging contrast is chip-confounded, R55/R91/R98/R116.    **Verdict — selection among cells: not tested as a distinct mechanism. Replication-coupled maintenance: not tested as a distinct mechanism.** Both fit some observations. The transplant, CH, culture share, and CR tests weaken simple predictions of selection. Solo-WCGW loss in Hannum and epiTOC1 point to replication-related change, but neither measures maintenance at protected promoters.  
   **Corrected sentence.** “Dividing cultures show a housekeeping gap in several designs, and some arrest arms had too little drift to estimate a share. Existing culture, clonal, transplant, and diet comparisons do not distinguish selection from replication-coupled maintenance.”
