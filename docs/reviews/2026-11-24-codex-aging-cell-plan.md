## Editorial judgment

v0.3 has a plausible *Aging Cell* paper in it. Its strongest result is that promoter expression measured in the same samples accounts for 71–82% of the housekeeping drift gap in three systems, with a gradient outside the predefined gene sets and a clear CD4 T-cell exception. The strongest follow-up would test whether this pattern reflects replication-coupled maintenance or selection among lineages. A clock screen and a pooled tissue ranking would add breadth while leaving that question open. I would drop D1’s proposed claim that age span and turnover explain the gap. [Draft](manuscript/paper.md:433) · [Aging Cell scope](https://www.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/page/journal/14749726/homepage/forauthors.html)

## B. Mechanism and the atlas

The local GSE292803 manifest has **108 allc entries, exactly one per 36 cell types at 8 weeks, 9 months and 18 months**. The local promoter extracts are Parquets, and the schema I checked has coordinates and methylated/total counts without cell or animal IDs. The series matrix was absent locally and I could not retrieve it. NCBI’s design record and the source paper identify multiple animals contributing to pooled regional replicates. That establishes pooling in the experiment, while the available files lack separately identifiable animals per cell type and age. Audit whether original cell metadata can recover independent **pools** before committing compute. With the files in hand, report descriptive trajectories only. Glial and neuronal labels are neither exchangeable nor measured proliferation states, so the proposed permutation and cell-type bootstrap cannot establish a division effect. [Local manifest](data/input/geo/GSE292803/filelist.txt) · [NCBI design](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/bioproject/PRJNA1241320) · [Atlas paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13007725/)

Prioritize R136’s six-donor division-versus-damage comparison, the paired colon–hippocampus result off the methylation floor, and R144’s paired WGBS contrast, which leaves the oligodendrocyte–neuron difference unresolved. In muscle stem cells, depth thinning strengthens the descriptive result, but `musc_single_cell` resamples cells within the same six mice. Label those intervals conditional on the mice and show mouse-level sensitivity. The missing discriminator is a donor-replicated lineage study measuring promoter methylation, expression, and lineage abundance over matched doublings. Maintenance predicts preservation within lineages. Selection predicts preferential loss or expansion associated with earlier promoter drift. A functional readout would strengthen the aging connection. Separate directed methylation change from rising dispersion, since relative entropy captures both, and distinguish 5mC from 5hmC if making a TET claim. ## A. Clocks and incremental value

**A1 is essential if clock comparisons remain.** `read_clock` drops intercepts, and `linear_score` omits absent probes and cohort-mean imputes sporadic missingness. A monotone nonlinear transformation can change age residuals. Validate the chosen scores against author implementations, including DunedinPACE’s normalization, and report missing absolute weight by reason and sample. A2 belongs in the supplement. R64 already found little evidence of compensatory weighting across most clocks. Separate CpG enrichment, absolute weight share, and slope-weighted signal. Current A2 code resamples CpGs, and `clock_weights` estimates unadjusted age slopes. Use gene blocks and donor refits where the statistic depends on estimated slopes. [Clock reader](src/entropy_age/data/clocks.py:67) · [Scoring code](src/entropy_age/experiments/shield_clocks.py:43) · [R64](docs/RUN_LOG.md:1322)

**A3 needs a new analysis specification.** Compare clinical and technical covariates plus one validated clock, then add housekeeping **B**, then **F given B** on identical observations. This separates the background-drift question from the shield question. Specify when F is estimable because its denominator is B. Centering a predictor within chip does not implement chip adjustment in logistic regression.

- **EPIC-Italy:** Use breast cancer diagnosed more than two years after draw as the explicit follow-up to R96. Preserve the original sampling and matching where recoverable, document reconstructed sets, verify control eligibility at the relevant follow-up, and resample matched sets. R96’s adjusted B was 1.11 (0.69–2.18), and the nominal F result appeared after rebuilding. Both need independent prospective validation. Absolute-risk calibration requires source-cohort sampling information or external validation. [R96](docs/RUN_LOG.md:928)
- **SJLIFE:** The local folder contains the deposited betas and a 5,013-sample matrix. GEO lists raw archives and paired IDATs, with none found locally. Audit first-draw linkage, treatment missingness and follow-up. Without last-contact dates for survivors, death by freeze is a descriptive binary endpoint with unequal observation windows. Keep the rebuild in the preprocessing program and off the submission’s critical path. - **Vitality 90+:** With 39 deaths, use a parsimonious Cox sensitivity analysis and check proportional hazards. Report cohort-specific estimates. Use likelihood tests for association and held-out or optimism-corrected performance for prediction. Fit references, imputation and scaling inside training resamples. Report paired performance intervals and calibration where identifiable, with a prespecified minimum worthwhile gain.

## C. Heterogeneity and fishing

Make the forest plot and provenance table. The approximately 30 rows mix platforms, probe universes, age windows, and study-specific SDs. `series.py` supplies marginal donor intervals. For shared donors or mice, jointly resample and refit all tissues to estimate covariance, then use paired contrasts or a multilevel model clustered by independent study. Restrict any meta-regression to comparable estimates and one or two moderators. Age span and turnover are already post hoc explanations. Test age span through fixed within-cohort age-window contrasts on a common scale. Two wide-age breast cohorts have little gap, and healthy and tumour-adjacent lung have opposite estimates. [Runner](src/entropy_age/experiments/series.py:141) · [Run log](docs/RUN_LOG.md:63)

Timestamp a protocol that lists previously inspected results and freezes eligibility, endpoint, lag, clock, score, preprocessing, covariates, contrasts, multiplicity and decision thresholds. Report every locked analysis. Reserve confirmation for untouched data. Searching clocks, lags, methylation bands or tissue classes for a publishable result would read as fishing.
