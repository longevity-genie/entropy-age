### Implementation

- **Reference, matching and resampling are correct for the stated statistic.** The code averages the two parental betas per CpG, computes regularized KL from each clone to that mean, and matches by island status × parental-beta decile. The weighted contrast is linear, so averaging CpG distances before scoring gives the same gap as averaging clone scores on fixed weights and scale. It resamples whole clones within strain and arm while holding the reference, strata and across-CpG SD fixed. The intervals are conditional on those choices and the two strains. Thirty draws give rough tail quantiles. Each arm has its own SD, so arm-to-arm effect comparisons need raw bits or a common denominator. - **The technical floor is a descriptive check.** Mean control distances are 0.039 and 0.043 bits, versus 0.0016 and 0.0013 for the parent pairs. Each pair gives one directional KL comparison across two chips, with its own SD and no uncertainty estimate. It uses a single array as reference, while clones use the mean of two. Even with independent equal-variance beta errors, those comparisons carry \(2\sigma^2\) and \(1.5\sigma^2\) of technical variance, respectively, before KL. ### What the design can resolve

A clone endpoint combines its founder’s inherited state, changes during expansion, and measurement error. Selection before cloning can already narrow housekeeping heterogeneity. Establishment and outgrowth can favor particular founders, and descendants can compete within a clone. The two bulk arrays estimate the parental mean and duplicate disagreement. They cannot measure the parental **cell-to-cell** distribution. The observed gap is therefore compatible with faithful copying of a founder distribution shaped by prior selection, with differential maintenance during expansion, or with both.

1. **Test a founder-only model.** On beta values, decompose the endpoint squared distance into the squared shift of the clone mean from the parent and variance among clones. Under random founding, faithful copying and equal establishment, the expected shift is zero and the biological between-clone variance equals parental between-cell variance. Simulate whole founders and array error, then apply the same matching and compare the observed gaps, shifts and dispersions. With these arrays, only sensitivity analyses are possible. A diploid founder has methylation fractions 0, ½ or 1 at a CpG, and the bulk mean does not determine their frequencies or cross-CpG dependence. Independent-allele sampling is one model assumption. Rejection would challenge that founder-only model. A fit would leave both mechanisms open.
2. **Use irradiation and stability as exploratory contrasts.** Estimate irradiated-minus-control and, among irradiated clones, unstable-minus-stable gaps within each strain on a common scale. Both treatment arms occur on every chip, so use within-chip contrasts and check sensitivity to either parent array. Stability is an endpoint karyotype label. Keep unknown labels explicit, mask affected copy-number regions in a sensitivity analysis, and verify whether `2103n` and `2103a` share a founder before resampling them independently. A contrast could reveal sensitivity to radiation or instability. Direct damage, treatment-dependent survival and outgrowth prevent either contrast from isolating maintenance. [Flunkert et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29964050/) · [GSE112812](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE112812) · [GSE112873](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE112873)
3. **To separate the mechanisms**, measure early sister branches and later descendants of the same founders at several doublings, and track lineage abundance in pooled versus separately grown cultures. The paired trajectories estimate within-lineage change. Abundance changes quantify selection among tracked lineages.
