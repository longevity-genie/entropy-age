# Young-adult blood reference for the young-SD question (P6)

Search of 28 November 2026. `level_match_reference` takes the matching variables (young mean level, young
between-donor SD) from another 450k blood cohort. With adult references (Hannum's youngest fifth, 19–52 y; EPIC-Italy's,
34–47 y) matching on young SD halves the housekeeping gap; with children (GSE97362 controls, 2–19 y) it does not. A
cohort of healthy donors aged 18–30 separates the two readings: low young SD set by early adulthood, or a property of
the adult references. Wanted: 450k, whole blood or PBMC, healthy donors mostly 18–30, n ≥ 50, raw IDATs preferred,
deposited betas acceptable; no disease-only cohorts; no donor overlap with Hannum, EPIC-Italy or GSE97362.

Metadata were read from each series' GEO record and series-matrix header, file sizes from the GEO download listing.
The earlier searches (`DATASET_SEARCH_2026-11-18.md` and the three of 2026-11-24) assessed no young-adult blood cohort.

**Result.** With the GSE73103 young adults as reference, matching on young SD halves the housekeeping gap as with the adult references, and their housekeeping CpGs vary less between donors than matched CpGs (0.18 percentage points). E-Risk, the second young-adult reference with another normalisation, gives a smaller young-SD difference (0.06 percentage points, against 0.03 in the GSE97362 children), and matching on its young SD removes about a third of the gap. At 100 donors per reference the differences stay (spread over draws at most 0.015 percentage points SD), so sample size does not explain them. Numbers in `docs/CHANGELOG.md` (2026-11-28, E-Risk entry) and `data/output/level_match_reference/`. Both cohorts were then rebuilt from their IDATs with the one SeSAMe pipeline (GSE73103 RAW.tar 3.0 GB, 185 of the 216 donors aged 18-30 passing QC; GSE105018 RAW.tar 13 GB, 923 of 924 families). On the same donors and CpGs the GSE73103-minus-E-Risk difference in housekeeping young SD is -0.110 percentage points (-0.123 to -0.093) deposited and -0.116 (-0.130 to -0.096) rebuilt (below zero = GSE73103's housekeeping CpGs spread less), so the difference belongs to the cohorts (Swedes aged 18-30, British twins aged exactly 18) and survives replacing the depositors' BMIQ and dasen with one SeSAMe pipeline. With every cohort rebuilt, matching on GSE73103's young SD removes about two thirds of the EPIC-Italy housekeeping gap and matching on E-Risk's about a third (`data/output/level_match_reference_rsesame/`). Open: which property of the cohorts sets the difference (age band, twin sampling, cell mix, array batches); IoPPN (GSE152027) controls would add a third young-adult cohort with raw arrays.
