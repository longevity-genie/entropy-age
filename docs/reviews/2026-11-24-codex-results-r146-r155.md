## Mouse verdict

**Heart is consistent with prior weak cardiac gaps. For liver, age and chip design plus array coverage are the leading explanations.** The rebuilt heart and field-liver files have the same 296,070 MM285 IDs, despite the heart’s GPL32543 GEO label. Each matches 71,178 runner candidates exactly. Complete-case filtering retains 66,709 and 64,404 probes, including 11,093 and 10,716 of 11,379 measurable housekeeping probes. They share 64,343 complete probes. Matching works as coded, but only **22 and 21** complete probes carry an island flag, versus 2,363 in BXD. The platforms share only 42 annotated promoter ID prefixes after normalization.

Heart’s nominal ages are 5 and 24 months. Coding two ages as 0/1 preserves the SD-scaled contrast and puts all 17 young mice in the reference. The 19 saline animals alone gave −0.021 in memory. BXD uses a ≤270-day reference for matching but the youngest fifth for KL. Restricting BXD to ≤302 days weakened its point estimate to −0.120, with only 5/200 usable draws.

The remaining discriminating run is **a broad-age C57BL/6J MM285 liver series with field and lab mice balanced across shared chips**, comparing the full span with ≤302 days on fixed CpGs, strata and scale. That would test age-window attenuation and an age-by-environment effect on the same platform.
