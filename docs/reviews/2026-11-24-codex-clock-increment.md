### What the results support

On the original matched sets, the join-corrected Biolearn refit gives **B OR 1.06** (quick 95% CI 0.64–2.25, LR p=.813) and **F OR 0.70** (0.507–1.002, p=.020). Neither meets P4’s joint rule. F is estimable with B: B was positive throughout and the model had full rank. At fixed B, higher F means a less negative gap and a weaker shield. The estimate therefore associates a weaker shield with *lower* case odds. The ratio imposes a B-dependent effect on the absolute gap P, so check sensitivity to `B + P` before interpreting it mechanistically. The reported disagreement correlations with B (.37) and F (.02) describe the current panel only.
