# Public methylation candidates for the ten underpowered findings

Repository audit completed 23 November 2026. I searched GEO with NCBI ESearch/ESummary (`db=gds`) and ArrayExpress with the BioStudies API, using assay, tissue, age and study-design terms. I checked the earlier verdicts and the in-hand list before ranking. Accession links lead to the repository records. PMIDs are the citations GEO attaches to each record, including where GEO attaches several. A title is given when the deposit has no PMID. Sample counts come from GEO Series Matrix metadata or ArrayExpress SDRFs, unless attributed to the series description. Sizes are decimal and refer to listed compressed file members for GEO and individual IDATs as listed by EBI. Archive wrappers, platform manifests, RNA data and sequencing reads are excluded unless explicitly identified. A filename check establishes public listing and naming. File contents and CpG coverage remain untested.

## What I could not check

- The 83 public subject labels in GSE330111 cannot be reconciled with the study-wide 103 from GEO alone. A numeric age key for the public labels, age distributions for the two CEAM deposits, and overlap of any new brain donors with the cohorts in hand remain unverified. The CEAM age check includes the publication’s [linked supplementary tables](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.29986714.v4).
- GSE184211 has per-specimen ages and chip IDs. The GEO README refers to a metadata spreadsheet, and the public supplement listing has no matching spreadsheet. Distinct people per tissue, shared people across tissues, health status and tissue-level promoter probe coverage remain unresolved.
- The series for prolonged culture arrest lacks the paired dividing trajectory, and I found no third independent series with a verified replicative-versus-damage comparison. The RAS series are useful only for a different induction mechanism.
- For most sequencing cohorts I could verify sample labels and listed CpG files. Lane-level balance, actual CpG coverage and nested archive contents remain unverified. Methylation bigWig resolution was inferred only where explicit filenames indicate a CpG call; otherwise it is marked unverified. I did not infer animal n from per-cell counts or equate RNA-library counts to donors.
- Exact mouse-to-cell and age linkage is missing in the public matrices for the blood dataset. The oocyte source table also omits the contributing mouse IDs. GSE330111 FASTQ files and GSE298243 FASTQ files are controlled access.
- A same-specimen key between the two lung expression/methylation deposits was unavailable in their GEO sample tables. The two hippocampus deposits share 39 exact donor IDs. The metadata do not establish a 40th pair. The myometrial and FTD joins use repository specimen/patient identifiers. Confirming the exact tissue aliquot would require finer metadata.
- I checked high-relevance hits from broad GEO and ArrayExpress searches. Some GEO directory requests returned 403/503 and some searches exceeded the returned hit window. Direct sample matrices and file manifests worked as a fallback for several accessions. Findings without a qualifying series here remain open to other public deposits or metadata releases.
